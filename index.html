Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Developed by EXOVYN

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Vice Codex handles information when you use our Android application.

Vice Codex is an independent gaming companion application providing game-related information, mission guides, trophy tracking, checklists, and progress management.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Vice Codex stores certain information locally on your device to provide its features, including:

  • Trophy completion progress.
  • Mission and guide completion progress.
  • Favorites and saved items.
  • Application settings and preferences.
  • Downloaded and cached game information.

This information supports application functionality and offline access. Your locally stored progress is not uploaded to our catalog servers.

3. Internet Access and Content Synchronization

Vice Codex uses an internet connection to retrieve and update game guides, trophies, missions, and other application content.

We use Supabase as our backend infrastructure for delivering and synchronizing this content.

When the application communicates with our backend, technical information such as IP addresses, request timestamps, and request metadata may be processed for service delivery, security, and troubleshooting.

Supabase Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

4. Personal Information

The current version of Vice Codex does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, or payment information to use its core features.

If you voluntarily contact us for support, we may receive your email address and the information you choose to provide.

5. How Information Is Used

Information is used for the following purposes:

  • Providing application functionality.
  • Delivering and updating game information.
  • Maintaining your locally stored progress.
  • Maintaining service security and reliability.
  • Responding to support and privacy inquiries.

6. Advertising and Analytics

The current version of Vice Codex does not contain third-party advertising or dedicated user-tracking analytics services.

We do not sell or rent users' personal information.

If future versions introduce advertising, analytics, or additional data collection, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

7. Data Sharing

Information may be processed by infrastructure providers, including Supabase, as necessary to operate and secure the application.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or necessary to protect the security of our services and users.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored application data remains on your device until you remove it, clear the application's storage, or uninstall the application, subject to Android backup settings.

To remove locally stored progress and settings:

  1. Open your Android device settings.
  2. Navigate to Apps.
  3. Select Vice Codex.
  4. Select Storage and clear application data.

Technical server logs are retained according to the configured retention settings of our infrastructure provider.

Support communications are retained only as reasonably necessary to resolve inquiries and meet applicable legal obligations.

You may contact us to request access to or deletion of personal information that we hold about you, subject to applicable law.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through Vice Codex, including encrypted network connections and restricted backend access.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

Vice Codex is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

11. Third-Party Links

Vice Codex may contain links to external websites or resources. Those services operate under their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices.
